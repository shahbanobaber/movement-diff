How it works

  1. Track. Your camera finds your shoulder, elbow and wrist in every frame, in your browser.
  2. Line up. Each reach is cut out and stretched to the same length, so fast and slow reaches compare point by point.
  3. Drift. How far your eyes-closed path sits from your eyes-open path, at each point of the reach.
  4. Wobble. How much your reaches vary around their own average.
  5. Check. Each difference is tested against chance by shuffling which set each reach came from.
